A row: flex

flex puts the children in a row. items-center aligns them vertically, justify-between pushes them to the two ends and gap-4 keeps space between them.

A stack: flex-col and gap

flex-col puts the children in a column. gap-3 sets the space between them and p-4 the space inside the border. max-w-xs limits the card width, and w-full makes the button as wide as the card.

Columns: grid and col-span

grid-cols-3 makes three equal columns. A child takes one column, and col-span-2 makes it take two: here, a nav and a wider content area.

Responsive: sm:, md:, lg:

Breakpoints are mobile first: a class with no prefix applies on every screen, and sm:grid-cols-2 applies from 40rem up. lg: starts at 64rem. mx-auto w-full max-w-3xl centers the content and limits its width.

A sticky header in a scrolling list

h-48 overflow-auto gives the list a fixed height and its own scroll bar. sticky top-0 keeps each letter at the top while its names scroll under it. bg-surface hides the names below.

On top: relative and absolute

absolute top-0 right-0 places the dot at the top right of the nearest relative parent, out of the flow. z-10 keeps it above its siblings.
